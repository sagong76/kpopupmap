Seongsu-dong is Seoul's pop-up capital: old factories and warehouses turned into rotating brand spaces, cafés and studios. Here's a relaxed **one-day plan** for your first visit.

## The plan

| Time | What to do | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | Arrive at **Seongsu Station** (Line 2), Exit 3 or 4 | Most pop-ups are within a 10-minute walk |
| 10:30 | **Your must-see pop-up first** | Lines are shortest right after opening |
| 12:00 | Lunch around **Yeonmujang-gil** | Eat before noon to skip restaurant queues |
| 13:30 | 2–3 more pop-ups | Pick walk-in ones — see the "No reservation needed" badge |
| 15:30 | Coffee break | Seongsu is famous for warehouse-style cafés |
| 16:30 | Walk to **Seoul Forest** | A green break; Seoul Forest Station (Suin-Bundang Line) is nearby |
| 18:00 | Dinner or head back | Seongsu → Myeongdong is ~20 min on Line 2 |

## Before you go

- **Check if you can book.** Many Korean booking apps need a Korean phone number. Every listing on this site shows whether **foreigners can book** or should **join the walk-in queue**.
- **Weekdays are much calmer** than weekends. If you can, visit Tuesday–Thursday.
- **Cards are accepted almost everywhere.** A T-money card makes the subway easy.
- **Stay connected.** You'll need maps, booking pages and queue notifications — get an eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi.
