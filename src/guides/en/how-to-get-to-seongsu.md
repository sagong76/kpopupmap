## Stations at a glance

| Station | Line | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Seongsu** | Line 2 | Yeonmujang-gil pop-up street. **Exits 3 & 4** are the pop-up hotspot |
| **Ttukseom** | Line 2 | West Seongsu café street |
| **Seoul Forest** | Suin-Bundang Line | Seoul Forest park, Atelier-gil |

> 💡 Most pop-ups cluster between **Seongsu Station Exits 3/4 and Yeonmujang-gil**. First time? Get off at Seongsu.

## From popular areas

- **Myeongdong / Euljiro** : Line 2 from Euljiro 1(il)-ga → Seongsu (no transfer, ~20 min)
- **Hongdae** : Line 2 from Hongik Univ. → Seongsu (no transfer, ~35–40 min)
- **Gangnam** : Line 2 from Gangnam → Seongsu (no transfer, ~20 min)

## From the airport

- **Incheon Airport** : AREX to Hongik Univ., transfer to Line 2 → Seongsu (~1 h 30 min)
- **Gimpo Airport** : AREX to Hongik Univ., transfer to Line 2

## Good to know

- Line 2 is a **loop line**. The wrong direction still gets you there, but it takes much longer.
- Get a T-money card at any convenience store for subways and buses.
- Seongsu Station gets very crowded on weekend afternoons.
