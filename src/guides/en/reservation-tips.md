## Four ways to get in

1. **Reservation required** : timed entry only. Booking openings are usually announced on the brand's Instagram.
2. **Reservation recommended** : reservations go first; walk-ins also allowed
3. **Walk-in queue** : register on a QR code or tablet on site and wait for your turn
4. **Free entry** : just line up

## What foreign visitors should know

> ⚠️ Many Korean booking apps (Naver Booking, etc.) require a **Korean mobile number**.

- Every listing on this site shows **"Foreigners can book"** or **"Foreigners: join the walk-in queue"**.
- If you can't book, **going on a weekday morning** is the most reliable option.
- If an on-site queue asks for a phone number, ask the staff. They can often help.

## Open-run tips

- Popular pop-ups get a line 30–60 minutes before opening.
- Limited merch often sells out on the first day or first weekend.
- The last day is the busiest. Look for the **"Last day"** badge on the map.
