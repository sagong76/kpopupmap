## Suggested route

1. Start at Seongsu Station Exit 3 and walk along **Yeonmujang-gil**
2. Lunch around the Seongsu café streets
3. In the afternoon, head toward **Seoul Forest** for the southern pop-ups

## Practical tips

- **Payment** : cards are accepted almost everywhere. Cash-only places are rare.
- **Internet** : get an eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi for maps and booking pages.
- **Luggage** : pop-ups are often small. Leave big bags at your hotel or a station locker.
- **Restrooms** : most pop-ups don't have one. Use cafés or subway stations.
- **Weather** : queues are often outdoors. Bring water and shade in summer, hand warmers in winter.

## How to use this site

- **Tap a date on the calendar** (left side, or bottom on mobile) to see only the pop-ups open that day.
- Toggle **categories** on and off to see what you like.
- Use **Add to calendar** to save the ones you want to visit.
